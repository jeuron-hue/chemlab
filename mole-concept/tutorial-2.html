6.T2 §1 · 04/10/26

Question 1

Balance these equations.

  1. Fe(s) + O2(g) → Fe2O3(s)
  2. C6H14(l) + O2(g) → CO2(g) + H2O(l)
  3. Al(s) + H2SO4(aq) → Al2(SO4)3(aq) + H2(g)
  4. Pb(NO3)2(s) → PbO(s) + NO2(g) + O2(g)

6.T2 §2 · 04/10/26

Question 2

Write balanced full and ionic equations, with state symbols, for these reactions.

  1. Aqueous silver nitrate is added to aqueous sodium chloride.
  2. Aqueous sodium carbonate is added to excess dilute hydrochloric acid.

6.T2 §3 · 04/10/26

Question 3

Limestone is heated to make lime: CaCO3(s) → CaO(s) + CO2(g).

  1. Calculate the mass of calcium oxide, in tonnes, that can be made from 1.00 tonne of calcium carbonate (1 tonne = 106 g).
  2. Calculate the volume of carbon dioxide released, at r.t.p.
  3. In a laboratory experiment, 10.0 g of calcium carbonate gave 5.00 g of calcium oxide. Calculate the percentage yield.

6.T2 §4 · 04/10/26

Question 4

3.00 g of magnesium is added to 80.0 cm3 of 1.00 mol dm−3 hydrochloric acid.

  1. Write the equation for the reaction.
  2. Show which reactant is in excess.
  3. Calculate the volume of hydrogen formed at r.t.p.
  4. Calculate the mass of the reactant left over.

6.T2 §5 · 04/10/26

Question 5

  1. Calculate the volume of oxygen needed to burn 50 cm3 of ethane completely, and the volume of carbon dioxide formed. All volumes are measured at the same temperature and pressure.
  2. 15 cm3 of a gaseous hydrocarbon was exploded with 120 cm3 of oxygen (an excess). After cooling to r.t.p. the volume was 90 cm3. After shaking with aqueous sodium hydroxide, the volume was 30 cm3. Find the formula of the hydrocarbon. Show that your answer is consistent with all three readings.

6.T2 §6 · 04/10/26

Question 6

  1. Calculate the mass of anhydrous sodium carbonate, Na2CO3, needed to make 250.0 cm3 of a 0.100 mol dm−3 standard solution.
  2. Describe how you would prepare this standard solution.
  3. Express the concentration in g dm−3.
  4. Calculate the volume of 2.00 mol dm−3 sulfuric acid needed to make 500 cm3 of 0.100 mol dm−3 sulfuric acid.

6.T2 §8 · 04/10/26

Question 8

An antacid tablet of mass 0.600 g contains magnesium hydroxide as its only base. The tablet was added to 50.0 cm3 of 0.500 mol dm−3 hydrochloric acid (an excess). After reaction, the solution was made up to 100.0 cm3. 25.0 cm3 portions needed a mean titre of 14.80 cm3 of 0.200 mol dm−3 sodium hydroxide.

  1. Explain why a back titration is used, rather than titrating the tablet directly.
  2. Write the equation for the reaction of magnesium hydroxide with hydrochloric acid.
  3. Calculate the percentage by mass of magnesium hydroxide in the tablet.

6.T2 §9 · 04/10/26

Question 9 (Further example)

6.00 g of impure iron(II) sulfate crystals, FeSO4·7H2O, was dissolved in dilute sulfuric acid and made up to 250.0 cm3. 25.0 cm3 portions needed a mean titre of 17.30 cm3 of 0.0200 mol dm−3 potassium dichromate(VI). The ionic equation is

Cr2O72−(aq) + 6Fe2+(aq) + 14H+(aq) → 2Cr3+(aq) + 6Fe3+(aq) + 7H2O(l)

  1. Show that the equation is balanced for charge.
  2. Calculate the percentage purity of the iron(II) sulfate crystals.

6.T2 §10 · 04/10/26

Question 10 (Further example)

Sodium thiosulfate solution is standardised with potassium iodate(V). 25.0 cm3 of 0.0200 mol dm−3 KIO3 was added to excess potassium iodide and dilute acid. The iodine formed needed 24.60 cm3 of the thiosulfate solution.

IO3−(aq) + 5I−(aq) + 6H+(aq) → 3I2(aq) + 3H2O(l)

I2(aq) + 2S2O32−(aq) → 2I−(aq) + S4O62−(aq)

  1. Deduce the mole ratio IO3− : S2O32−.
  2. Calculate the concentration of the sodium thiosulfate solution.
  3. Name the indicator, state when it is added, and give the colour change at the end point.

6.T2 §11 · 04/10/26

Question 11

  1. 0.0100 mol of a metal chloride, MCln, was dissolved in water, and excess aqueous silver nitrate was added. 4.30 g of silver chloride was precipitated. Deduce n.
  2. 25.0 cm3 of a 0.0500 mol dm−3 solution of an acid needed 25.00 cm3 of 0.100 mol dm−3 sodium hydroxide for complete neutralisation. Deduce the number of moles of H+ each mole of the acid can donate.
