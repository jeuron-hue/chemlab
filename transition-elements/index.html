    Copper(II) and ligand exchange

    Start with copper(II) sulfate solution, which contains [Cu(H2O)6]2+. Add aqueous ammonia, or concentrated hydrochloric acid, and watch the colour and the complex change. One ligand replaces another when the new complex is more stable; the equilibria shift with concentration (Le Chatelier, Topic 9).

            Transition elements as catalysts

            A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with lower activation energy. Transition elements are good catalysts for two reasons: variable oxidation states (the ion accepts and gives back electrons, forming an intermediate and being regenerated), and vacant or partly filled 3d orbitals that let reactant molecules be adsorbed on the metal surface.
