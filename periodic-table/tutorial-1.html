5.T1 §1 · 07/10/26

Question 1

  1. Write the full electronic configurations of Mg, Cl, Mg2+ and Cl−.
  2. State how the outer electronic configuration changes (i) across Period 3 from Na to Cl, and (ii) down Group 17 from Cl to I.

5.T1 §2 · 07/10/26

Question 2

  1. Explain why the atomic radius decreases from sodium to chlorine.
  2. Explain why the atomic radius increases from magnesium to barium.

5.T1 §3 · 07/10/26

Question 3

Use the Data Booklet values of atomic and ionic radii to answer this question.

  1. Explain why the Na+ ion is smaller than the Na atom.
  2. Explain why the Cl− ion is larger than the Cl atom.
  3. Explain why the radius of the Si4+ ion (0.041 nm) is so much smaller than that of the P3− ion (0.212 nm).
  4. (Further example) Arrange the ions N3−, O2−, Na+ and Al3+ in order of increasing radius, and explain your order.

5.T1 §4 · 07/10/26

Question 4

  1. Explain why the first ionisation energy of chlorine is much greater than that of sodium.
  2. Explain why the first ionisation energy of aluminium is lower than that of magnesium.
  3. Explain the decrease in first ionisation energy from Cl (1260) to Br (1140) to I (1010 kJ mol−1).

5.T1 §5 · 07/10/26

Question 5

  1. Define the term electronegativity.
  2. Describe and explain the trend in electronegativity (i) across Period 3 and (ii) down Group 2.

5.T1 §6 · 07/10/26

Question 6

The graph shows the melting points of eight elements, A to H, the consecutive elements of Period 3, in order of increasing proton number.

  1. Which element has a giant molecular structure? Explain your choice.
  2. Explain why D has a much higher melting point than E.
  3. Explain why C has a higher melting point than A.
  4. Explain why F has a higher melting point than E.
  5. Which of the elements A to H conduct electricity well as solids? Explain why.
  6. Which of A to H is a noble gas? Explain your answer using the graph.

5.T1 §7 · 07/10/26

Question 7

Sketch a graph of the melting points of the Period 3 elements from sodium to argon. Label the parts of the graph with the type of structure of the elements.

5.T1 §8 · 07/10/26

Question 8

  1. State the physical states of chlorine, bromine and iodine at room temperature and pressure.
  2. Explain the trend in volatility down Group 17.
  3. A student says “iodine has the highest boiling point of the three because the I–I bond is the strongest.” Use the Data Booklet to show that the student is wrong, and explain why the bond energy is not relevant to the boiling point.

5.T1 §9 · 07/10/26

Question 9

  1. Explain why aluminium is a better electrical conductor than sodium.
  2. Explain why sulfur does not conduct electricity.
  3. Silicon has the same structure as diamond. State the type of structure and explain why silicon is not a good conductor, unlike aluminium next to it.
