Explorer · 9476 11.4(b)(i), (c)

Markovnikov carbocation explorer

When HX adds to an unsymmetrical alkene the H atom can go to either carbon of the C=C, so two carbocations, and two products, are possible. Both form; the one from the more stable carbocation is the major product. Mechanisms are in Topic 11.3; this applies them (Part 1 of the notes, Markovnikov's rule).

    Detective · 9476 11.4(b)(iii)–(iv)

    KMnO4 cleavage detective

    Hot, acidified KMnO4 breaks the C=C completely. Judge each carbon of the old double bond on its own: =CH2 gives CO2 + H2O; =CHR gives a carboxylic acid, RCOOH; =CRR′ gives a ketone, RCOR′. Work forwards from an alkene, or backwards from the products.
