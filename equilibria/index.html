Explorer · 9476 9(a)

Dynamic equilibrium in a closed box

A reversible reaction goes both ways at once. Watch the forward and reverse rates: as the reactants are used up the forward rate falls, as the products build up the reverse rate rises, until the two are equal. From then on the amounts stay constant, but both reactions carry on.

      Drill · 9476 9(d)

      Writing Kc and Kp

      Products over reactants, each raised to the power of its coefficient. Pure solids and pure liquids are left out, and so is water when it is the solvent of a dilute solution. Kp uses partial pressures, so it is written only for gases; units follow from the expression.
