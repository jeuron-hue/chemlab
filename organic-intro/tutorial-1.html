Question 1

Compound X contains 64.9 % carbon, 13.5 % hydrogen and 21.6 % oxygen by mass. Its relative molecular mass is 74. (Ar: C 12.0, H 1.0, O 16.0.)

  1. Calculate the empirical formula of X.
  2. Deduce the molecular formula of X.
  3. X is a secondary alcohol. Name X and give its structural, displayed and skeletal formulae.
  4. Name an isomer of X that is a tertiary alcohol.

Question 2

Name the following compounds.

  1. CH3CH2CH(CH3)CH2OH
  2. CH3CHClCH2CH3
  3. CH3COCH2CH3
  4. CH3CH2COOCH3
  5. CH3CH2CH2CN
  6. CH3CH2CONH2
  7. (CH3)2CHCHO
  8. CH3CH2COCl
  9. C6H5OH
  10. CH3CH2NH2

Question 3

Draw skeletal formulae for: (a) 3-methylbut-1-ene; (b) 2,2-dichloropropane; (c) pentan-3-one; (d) ethyl methanoate; (e) 2-aminopropanoic acid; (f) 1,4-dimethylbenzene.

Question 4

A σ bond is said to be “symmetrical about the internuclear axis”. Is the same true of a π bond? Explain, and state what this means for rotation about a double bond.

Question 5

Draw a complete orbital picture to show the bonding in allene (propadiene), CH2=C=CH2. Your picture should clearly show the 3D shape of the molecule. Clearly indicate the types of orbitals involved (s, p, sp, sp2, sp3) and the types of bonds formed. Finally, use your picture to decide whether the four hydrogen atoms are in the same plane. If not, what is their spatial relationship?

Question 6

What orbitals overlap to form the bond that connects the methyl group to the double bond in propene, H2C=CH–CH3?

Question 7

One of the first drugs to be approved for the treatment of AIDS was azidothymidine (AZT), shown below.

  1. How many carbon atoms are sp3 hybridised?
  2. How many carbon atoms are sp2 hybridised?
  3. Which atom is sp hybridised?
  4. How many σ bonds are in the molecule?
  5. How many π bonds are in the molecule?
  6. What is the N–N–N bond angle in the N=N=N group?
  7. What is the H–O–C bond angle in the –CH2OH group attached to the five-membered ring?
  8. What is the hybridisation of the oxygen atom in the –CH2OH group?

Question 8

For each molecule, state the hybridisation of each carbon atom and predict the bond angles around it. For (b), also predict the H–N–H bond angle.

  1. methanal, HCHO
  2. methylamine, CH3NH2
  3. propyne, CH3C≡CH
  4. buta-1,3-diene, CH2=CH–CH=CH2. How many σ and how many π bonds does it have?
