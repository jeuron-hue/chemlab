    Curly arrows

    A full-headed arrow moves a pair of electrons; a half-headed (fish-hook) arrow moves one electron. Every arrow starts at a lone pair, a bond or a negative charge, and points to where the electrons go: from an electron-rich site to an electron-poor one.     Draw as
    • Stem: the longest carbon chain that contains the functional group (meth, eth, prop, but, pent, hex). The C of –CHO, –COOH, –COCl, –CONH2 and –C≡N counts in the chain.
    • Suffix: the highest-priority group: acid > ester > acyl chloride > amide > nitrile > aldehyde > ketone > alcohol > amine.
    • Locants: number from the end that gives the suffix group the lowest number, then the C=C, then the prefixes.
    • Prefixes in alphabetical order (ignore di-, tri-).
