The tri-iodomethane test

Acidity ladder: water, phenol and ethanol

All three are Brønsted-Lowry acids: each donates a proton from an O–H group to water. The order of acid strength is ethanol < water < phenol. Pick a compound and a reagent to see how the order shows up in the test tube, then compare the conjugate bases to see why.

    Explorer · 9476 11.6(d)(iii)

    Ring reactions: phenol against benzene

    One lone pair on the oxygen of phenol is delocalised into the ring. This raises the electron density of the ring, most of all at positions 2, 4 and 6, so phenol reacts with electrophiles under far milder conditions than benzene. Pick a ring and a reagent.
