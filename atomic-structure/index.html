Beam simulator · 1(a)–(b)

Beams in an electric field

Beams of particles enter the field between two charged plates at the same speed. Positive particles bend towards the negative plate, negative particles towards the positive plate, and neutrons go straight on. For beams at the same speed, the angle of deflection is proportional to charge/mass.

Predict · 1(b)

Predict the deflection

Angle of deflection ∝ charge/mass, for beams entering at the same speed. Work out charge/mass for the new particle, compare it with the proton's (1 ÷ 1 = 1), and scale the proton's angle.

Configuration builder · 1(h)

Electronic configurations

Choose an element and a charge, then fill the orbitals one electron at a time. Each electron goes into the lowest-energy orbital available (aufbau); each orbital holds at most two electrons, of opposite spin (Pauli); orbitals in the same subshell are filled singly, with parallel spins, before any pairing (Hund).

Successive ionisation energies · 1(j)–(k)

Successive ionisation energies

The nth ionisation energy is the energy for X(n−1)+(g) → Xn+(g) + e−. Each one is larger than the last, and a big jump shows the next electron comes from a shell closer to the nucleus.

Outer-shell configuration?
