Explorer · 9813 1.5(b)(ii)

Isotope patterns

See where the M+2 and M+4 peaks of chlorine and bromine compounds come from, and count carbon atoms from the M+1 peak. Bar heights are calculated from the isotope abundances (literature values, as in Topic 1): ¹²C 98.9 %, ¹³C 1.1 %; ³⁵Cl 75.8 %, ³⁷Cl 24.2 %; ⁷⁹Br 50.7 %, ⁸¹Br 49.3 %. The tallest peak of each cluster is set to 100.

Halogen atoms in the ion
Mode

Carbon-13 and the number of carbon atoms

Each carbon atom has about a 1.1 % chance of being ¹³C, so the M+1 peak is about 1.1 % of the M peak per carbon atom.
