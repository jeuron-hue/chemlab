9476 11.7(b)

Which hydroxynitriles are chiral?

Pick a carbonyl compound. The chirality is worked out from the product's structure: the carbon that was the carbonyl carbon is a chiral centre when its four groups are all different.

The rule: the hydroxynitrile is chiral whenever the two groups on the carbonyl carbon are different (every aldehyde except methanal, and every ketone whose two groups differ).

    Which double bond is reduced?

    Propenal, CH2=CHCHO, has a C=C and a C=O. Choose the reducing agent.

    Enrichment

    How the hydrides work

    LiAlH4 (and NaBH4, which is beyond the 9476 syllabus) act as a source of the hydride ion, H−, a nucleophile. It adds to the δ+ carbonyl carbon exactly as CN− does, and the π electrons move onto the oxygen. The O− is protonated when water is added:

    R2C=O + H− → R2CH–O−
    R2CH–O− + H2O → R2CH–OH + OH−

    That is why hydrides reduce the polar C=O bond but not a non-polar C=C bond. The mechanism is beyond the 9476 syllabus.
