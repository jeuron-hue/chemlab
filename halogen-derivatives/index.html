      Sort and predict · 11.5(a)

      Substitution or elimination?

      The same hydroxide ion can be a nucleophile or a base: the solvent decides. Sort each reaction by what happens, then predict products from a random halogenoalkane and reagent.

      What happens in each reaction? Drag each card into a box (use the dotted grip on a touch screen), or tap a card and then tap a box. Then press Check.

      • NaOH(aq), heat: OH− in water acts mainly as a nucleophile: an alcohol.
      • KCN in ethanol, heat: CN− attacks through C: a nitrile with one more carbon atom.
      • excess NH3 in ethanol, heat in a sealed tube: a primary amine.
      • NaOH in ethanol, heat: OH− acts mainly as a base: an alkene (secondary and tertiary halogenoalkanes).
      • Halogenoarenes and fluoroalkanes do not react under these conditions.
