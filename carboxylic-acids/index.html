Acyl chloride hub

An acyl chloride reacts with any H–Nu that has a lone pair: the –Cl is replaced by –Nu and HCl is lost, at room temperature. Tap a reagent. Predict mode generates questions with other acyl chlorides and reagents.

Bench · 9476 11.8(d), (f)

Ease of hydrolysis: acyl, alkyl and aryl chlorides

A few drops of each chloride in aqueous silver nitrate with a little ethanol (to dissolve the organic liquid): room temperature first, then warm in a water bath. Any Cl− released by hydrolysis gives a white precipitate of AgCl. 1-Chlorobutane is used rather than chloroethane, which is a gas at room temperature (b.p. 12 °C, literature). Times on the clock are illustrative.
