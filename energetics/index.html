      Sorter · 9476 7(g)–(i)

      Entropy: which way does disorder go?

      Entropy, S, is a measure of the disorder of a system: the number of ways its particles and their energy can be arranged. Gases have far more disorder than liquids, and liquids more than solids. A quantitative treatment is not required.

      ΔG⦵ = ΔH⦵ − TΔS⦵ explorer
